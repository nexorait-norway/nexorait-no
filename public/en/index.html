OUR APPROACH

Simple, written and transparent

1. Tell us the problem. Email a short description of your current workflow and the tools involved.

2. Receive a written assessment. We review feasibility, scope, dependencies and any relevant data protection requirements.

3. Agree on a pilot. If feasible, we propose a defined scope, price, acceptance criteria and delivery plan before any commitment.

4. Test before rollout. The solution must pass appropriate functional and security checks before use with real business data.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is Nexorait a ready-made software platform?

Not yet. We currently focus on individually scoped setups and pilots based on available tools and integrations.

Do we need a meeting or phone call?

No. Initial enquiries, scoping and proposals are handled in writing.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

It depends on your systems, their available APIs and access permissions. We verify this before making a commitment.

Can you work with businesses outside Norway?

International enquiries are welcome. Service availability depends on the project's technical, contractual, privacy and regulatory requirements.
